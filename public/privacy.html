Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Deft Decor uses the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API, provided by Meta Platforms, to message customers who have opted in. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Information we process

  • WhatsApp phone numbers of customers who message us or opt in
  • Message content, templates, delivery status, and timestamps
  • Display names provided by WhatsApp
  • Opt-in / opt-out and block status

How we use it

We use this information only to operate customer support and service chats on WhatsApp, send opted-in notifications, and keep the account within WhatsApp messaging policies. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

Messages are transmitted through Meta / WhatsApp Cloud API. We do not share customer chats with unrelated third parties except when required by law.

Retention

Conversation history is stored so our team can reply. Customers can ask us to delete their chat data. Sending STOP on WhatsApp opts the number out of further template messages.

Contact

For privacy requests related to WhatsApp messaging, contact Deft Decor through the same WhatsApp business thread or the Meta Business Suite account that owns this app.
